    Instructions

    • Survey Setup

      Navigate to your survey location and enter all metadata before beginning. For BBS, orient to true north to accurately estimate observation positions. The toggleable distance/bearing overlay (concentric rings at 50 m, 100 m, 150 m, 200 m with cardinal directions) assists with spatial placement. Range and bearing are calculated relative to your current GPS position.

    • Recording Observations

      Tap the map at the estimated location of the observation. BBS: Use the species search bar (common name or 4-letter code) to identify species. Moose: Select species and observation type from the dropdowns. Turtle: Record individual or habitat observations with the relevant fields. Observations are not saved until you tap "Save Observation".

    • Editing & Deleting

      Tap any map marker to open its edit popup, or use the 🔍 icon in the data drawer. Delete individual records with the ❌ button in the drawer or the Delete button in the popup.

    • Data Storage & Export

      All observations are cached in a local IndexedDB database for offline use. Current-session markers are larger and brighter; archived markers are smaller and dark. Export CSV, GeoJSON, or KML from the data drawer. Each survey type has its own export prefix (BBS_, MOOSE_, TURTLE_).
